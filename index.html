Who We Are

Dedicated to Empowering Coastal Communities

Pwani Circular Economy Association (PCEA) is a grassroots NGO committed to transforming lives along Kenya's coast. We believe in sustainable development that puts people first—fostering resilience, restoring ecosystems, and building economies that thrive for generations.

Our Mission

To uplift communities through innovative circular economy solutions, education, and environmental stewardship.

Our Vision

A prosperous, resilient coastal Kenya where communities live in harmony with a healthy environment.

What We Do

Our Focus Areas

Holistic programs designed to create lasting change

🎓

Education

Quality learning opportunities and scholarship programs for children and youth in coastal communities.

💧

Clean Water

Building and repairing wells, rain harvesting systems, and water purification infrastructure.

🌾

Food Security

Promoting sustainable agriculture, kitchen gardens, and nutrition education to combat hunger.

♻️

Circular Economy

Waste-to-wealth initiatives, recycling cooperatives, and sustainable livelihood training.

🤝

Community Empowerment

Building local leadership, self-help groups, and economic resilience through skills training.

🌿

Environmental Conservation

Mangrove restoration, coastal cleanups, and biodiversity protection for future generations.
