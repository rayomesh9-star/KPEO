Who We Are

Driving Change Along Kenya's Coast

PCEA is a non-profit organization dedicated to transforming the Pwani region through innovative circular economy solutions, community-led development, and environmental stewardship.

Our Mission

To catalyze sustainable development along Kenya's coast by implementing circular economy models that reduce waste, conserve natural resources, and uplift marginalized communities.

Our Vision

A thriving, self-sustaining coastal region where economic growth and environmental conservation coexist harmoniously.

What We Do

Our Focus Areas

We address critical challenges through targeted interventions in environment, education, water, and community empowerment.

🌿

Environment

Mangrove restoration, coastal conservation, and ecosystem rehabilitation projects.

💧

Clean Water

Providing sustainable access to clean drinking water through rainwater harvesting and well drilling.

📚

Education

Scholarships, school infrastructure, and environmental awareness programs for youth.

🌾

Food Security

Promoting climate-smart agriculture and sustainable farming practices.

Why PCEA

Transparency, Impact, and Community-Led Change

We believe sustainable change can only come from within communities. Every initiative we launch is co-designed with local leaders and beneficiaries.
