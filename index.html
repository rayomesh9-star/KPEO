Who We Are

Kyeni Poverty Eradication Organization (KPEO)

Organization Type: Local Non-Governmental Organization (NGO)

Founded: 2003

Tagline: Eradicating Poverty, Restoring Hope.

KPEO is a community-driven organization dedicated to eradicating poverty and improving livelihoods across Kenya. Through education, health, agriculture, and community empowerment programs, we create sustainable change for vulnerable households, women, youth, and rural communities.

Why KPEO

Transparency, Impact, and Community-Led Change

We believe sustainable change can only come from within communities. Every initiative we launch is co-designed with local leaders and beneficiaries.
