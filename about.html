About Us

Our Story of Change and Resilience

For over a decade, KPEO has been at the forefront of sustainable development along Kenya's beautiful coast.

Our Story

From a Small Idea to a Regional Movement

Founded in 2012 by a group of passionate coastal conservationists, PCEA began as a community-based initiative addressing plastic waste in Mombasa's beaches. Today, we operate across Kilifi, Mombasa, and Lamu counties, impacting over 12,500 lives.

Our journey has been one of resilience, learning, and unwavering commitment to the circular economy principles that guide our work.

Our Mission

To eradicate poverty by expanding access to education, driving food security, improving access to clean water, and empowering communities to become self-reliant.

Our Vision

A future where every individual and family lives free from poverty, with access to quality education, dignified employment, clean water, and equal opportunities to thrive.

"Eradicating Poverty. Reforming Lives."

Our Impact

What We Do (Our Programs)

We deliver holistic, community-centered programs across education, livelihoods, health, environment, and empowerment.

University and College Sponsorships

We identify academically deserving but financially disadvantaged students and support them in accessing universities, colleges, and technical institutions through scholarships, sponsorships, mentorship, and educational assistance.

Education Support

We promote quality education by providing learning materials, mentoring learners, supporting school improvement initiatives, and encouraging school retention among vulnerable children.

Poverty Eradication and Livelihood Development

We empower vulnerable households through income-generating activities, entrepreneurship training, savings groups, and skills development to improve family incomes and reduce dependency.

Food Security and Climate-Smart Agriculture

We promote sustainable farming practices, drought-resistant crops, kitchen gardening, water harvesting, and improved livestock production to enhance food security and nutrition.

Women Empowerment

We strengthen the economic and social well-being of women through entrepreneurship training, financial literacy, leadership development, and support for small businesses and self-help groups.

Youth Empowerment

We equip young people with vocational skills, life skills, leadership training, entrepreneurship opportunities, and career guidance to prepare them for productive and independent lives.

Health and Community Well-being

We conduct health education, promote sanitation and hygiene, support maternal and child health, and organize community health awareness campaigns to improve overall well-being.

Environmental Conservation

We encourage tree planting, environmental education, climate change awareness, land restoration, and sustainable management of natural resources to protect the environment for future generations.

Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene (WASH)

We promote access to safe water, proper sanitation facilities, and good hygiene practices to reduce waterborne diseases and improve community health.

Community Capacity Building

We strengthen the capacity of community groups, local leaders, and institutions through training, mentorship, and leadership development to promote sustainable community-driven development.

How We Work

Our Approach

Circular economy is at the heart of everything we do. We close loops, reduce waste, and create value from resources that would otherwise be discarded.

🔄

Reduce

Minimize waste generation through community education and sustainable practices.

♻️

Reuse

Create value from discarded materials through innovation and upcycling programs.

♻️

Restore

Heal ecosystems through mangrove restoration, coastal cleanup, and reforestation.

Engagement

Community-Led Development

We believe communities are the experts of their own needs. PCEA works alongside local leaders, women's groups, youth cooperatives, and village elders to design and implement solutions that are culturally appropriate and economically viable.

From participatory budgeting to community monitoring committees, our engagement model ensures sustainability long after our projects formally end.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does PCEA ensure transparency? +

All financial records are published quarterly on our website, and we undergo annual independent audits to maintain full transparency with donors and the community.

Can I volunteer directly with community projects? +

Yes! We organize scheduled volunteer programs throughout the year. Please reach out through our contact page to express interest and find the right fit for your skills and availability.

How are partner organizations selected? +

We select partners based on alignment with our values, capacity for impact, and proven track record in community-based environmental or social programs.

Is my donation tax-deductible? +

Yes. PCEA is a registered non-profit organization and issues official tax-deductible receipts for all donations over KES 500.
